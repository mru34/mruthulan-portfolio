What I built

Before and after

The whole job was removing a conversation.

Before: WhatsApp

  • Message the shop to ask what is free.
  • Wait for a reply.
  • Agree a time.
  • Confirm again closer to the day.

After: the site

  • Open the site.
  • Pick an open slot.
  • Confirm.
