Better Call Bhai

Barber bookings, minus the back-and-forth.

Booking a haircut should not take three messages and a phone call. This site lets a customer pick a service, a day and an open slot, then confirm on WhatsApp. It is deployed on Render and the shop is piloting it; most bookings still come in through WhatsApp for now.

My role
Web design, frontend build and deployment
Status
Deployed pilot
Stack
HTML · CSS · JavaScript · hosted on Render
Client
A local barbershop in Singapore

Before and after

The whole job was removing a conversation.

Before: WhatsApp

  • Message the shop to ask what is free.
  • Wait for a reply.
  • Agree a time.
  • Confirm again closer to the day.

After: the site

  • Open the site.
  • Pick an open slot.
  • Confirm.
