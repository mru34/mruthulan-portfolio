Before and after

The whole job was removing a conversation.

Before — WhatsApp

  • Message the shop to ask what is free.
  • Wait for a reply.
  • Agree a time.
  • Confirm again closer to the day.

After

  • Open the site.
  • Pick an open slot.
  • Confirm.

Evidence

The booking flow, end to end.

Captured from the site’s own code running locally with an empty database, so no customer appears. The struck-through times are test bookings.

My role

I owned the customer journey, the appointment form, the mobile interface and the Render deployment, replacing manual WhatsApp appointment coordination.

Result

Live, and in use by a real business.
