Privacy / Analytics

A simple choice
about analytics.

This portfolio loads Google Analytics 4 only when you choose “Allow analytics.” It helps me understand which work people read and how they find the site.

What is measured

Google Analytics may collect page views, approximate geographic location, referral source, browser and device information, and interactions such as outbound clicks, which project you open, and whether you used site search or the contact options. Search never sends what you typed, only which result you opened. It uses cookies or similar browser identifiers. I use aggregate reports to improve the site, and I do not ask you to create an account here.

Your choice

The analytics script does not load until you opt in. Your choice is saved in this browser so you are not asked on every page. If you decline, the site still works normally. You can change your choice at any time.
