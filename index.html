03 / About

I build, test, explain
and ship software.

I care about the last mile: whether someone can actually use the result, whether it survives failure, and whether I can explain the decisions clearly.

I enjoy turning rough ideas into products people can actually try. Hackathons taught me how to make decisions quickly, build around real user and technical constraints, and explain my work clearly under pressure. In my next internship, I want to contribute to production software, learn from experienced engineers and become a stronger full-stack developer.

  • I build and ship real products.

    Better Call Bhai is live and taking bookings for a real business. On SignalBridge, the automated tests and edge cases were part of my contribution.

  • I design around actual users and technical constraints.

    MEANT keeps AAC and typing available even if its AI layer goes down. Loomy began with more than 30 interviews by the team.

  • I communicate through presentations and student leadership.

    I presented MEANT at the Dell InnovateFest national final, and as class chairman I handle class communication and student outreach.
