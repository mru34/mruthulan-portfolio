03 / About

I start from the person,
not the technology.

Then I finish it and put it somewhere real. That is the part most student projects skip, and it is the part I care about.

It shows up in what I work on. SignalBridge hands the decision back to the youth worker instead of making it for them. MEANT suggests a reply, but the AAC user picks every word that gets spoken. Better Call Bhai is not a demo — it takes real bookings for a real barber shop.

I test it

On SignalBridge the automated tests were mine. Edge cases are cheaper to find than to explain on stage.

I ship it

Deployment is not someone else’s job. If it is not live, it is not finished.

I start with people

Loomy began with more than 30 interviews before a single screen was drawn.
