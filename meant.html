One turn

Three seconds of a conversation.

  1. The room moves onTurn Claim tells the other person an answer is coming.
  2. A reply is offeredSuggestions come from a Dell GB10. Nothing leaves the device.
  3. The user authors itThey pick, type or use their board. Nothing is spoken without a tap.
