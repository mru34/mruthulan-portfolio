01 / The challenge

Conversations do not wait.

AAC users need time to answer. A conversation that keeps moving leaves that time behind.

02 / The idea

Prediction, with a clear final say.

Local context produces timely reply suggestions. The user chooses one, types, or uses their AAC board. Nothing is spoken without a tap.

“Turn Claim” tells the other person an answer is coming.

03 / How it works

Intelligence that stays in the room.

A Dell GB10 runs the speech, context and reply services locally.

If the AI layer stops, AAC and typing still work.
