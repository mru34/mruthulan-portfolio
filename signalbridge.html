SignalBridge.

Better handoffs for youth support.

A consent-led platform built for a Singapore Children’s Society challenge.

RECOGNITIONSP InnovateDash 2026 Champion
MY FOCUSYouth UX, integrations and testing
STACKNext.js · FastAPI · PostgreSQL

01 / THE PROBLEM

Support should carry context forward.

After-hours conversations can leave workers without the context they need.

SignalBridge makes the next handoff clearer while workers keep the final judgement.

02 / THE PRODUCT

One place for a careful handoff.

One place for conversations, risk signals and consent-led briefs.

AI assists. Youth workers decide.
