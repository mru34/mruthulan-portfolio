The handoff

Five moments, and the judgement stays with the worker at every one.

01 · Message

A young person writes in, out of hours, to whoever is on shift.

02 · Signal

The system flags risk signals in the thread. It does not act on them.

03 · Consent

Nothing is passed on until consent is explicit.

04 · Brief

AI drafts the handoff summary. The worker edits, approves or discards it.

05 · Worker

The next shift opens with context instead of a cold thread.

Decisions I made

Two calls that were mine to make.

Could have

Shipped the demo path only — it was a competition.

I did

Wrote the automated tests around the handoff logic.

Could have

Asked the young person to install one more app.

I did

Built the Discord integration, so the conversation stayed where it already was.
