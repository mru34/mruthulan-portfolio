The handoff

Five moments, and the judgement stays with the worker at every one.

  1. MessageA young person writes in, out of hours, to whoever is on shift.
  2. SignalThe system flags risk signals in the thread. It does not act on them.
  3. ConsentNothing is passed on until consent is explicit.
  4. BriefAI drafts the handoff summary. The worker edits, approves or discards it.
  5. WorkerThe next shift opens with context instead of a cold thread.
